Artificial Intelligence Use Notice — EventOS

You are interacting with artificial intelligence

Certain features of EventOS, including those identified as AI Agents or other assisted features, may use third-party artificial intelligence systems and language models to generate responses, summaries, suggestions, classifications, drafts or other results.

Current providers: Google (Gemini), Anthropic (Claude) and OpenAI.

Where applicable, EventOS will clearly inform the user that they are interacting with an AI system.

What information may be sent

When you use an AI feature, EventOS may transmit to the provider the information necessary to execute the request, which may include:

  • the text or prompt entered;
  • selected context from EventOS;
  • files or images that the user chooses to attach;
  • instructions necessary to produce the result.

You should not enter sensitive or confidential data that is not necessary for the task or that you are not authorized to process.

Use of data by providers

How a provider retains or uses information depends on the product, configuration and contract that JBD has in effect with that provider. The Privacy Policy and this notice will be updated if those conditions change materially.

Limitations of AI

Results may be incorrect, incomplete, biased, inconsistent or outdated. AI may generate information that seems convincing but is not true.

The user must apply appropriate human review before using a result for:

  • commitments with customers or suppliers;
  • prices, quotes or financial decisions;
  • legal or regulatory decisions;
  • security;
  • employment decisions or decisions about people;
  • any decision with relevant consequences.

EventOS does not guarantee the accuracy of AI-generated results.

Automated decisions

Unless expressly stated otherwise in a specific feature, EventOS's generative AI features are designed as assistance tools and not as an automatic substitute for human decision-making.

If in the future EventOS implements systems that make or recommend decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on people, we will assess and apply the additional obligations that correspond before their deployment.

Transparency

EventOS endeavors to identify AI interactions in a clear and distinguishable manner where applicable. Certain synthetic content may additionally require specific marking, labeling or disclosures in accordance with applicable regulations and the type of content.

Personal data of third parties

The customer organization is responsible for having sufficient authorization or legal basis to enter personal data of third parties into EventOS and to use it in AI features.

We recommend avoiding health information, government identifiers, detailed financial data, credentials, trade secrets or other highly sensitive data unless strictly necessary, authorized and the feature has been approved for that use.

Ownership and use of output

Rights over inputs and outputs are governed by the EventOS Terms of Service and, where applicable, by the applicable terms of the underlying providers. The use of AI does not guarantee that a result is unique or free of third-party rights.
