## 1. Who we are

EventOS is a software-as-a-service (SaaS) platform for managing the
production and operation of events, operated by **JBD Investment Corp
Inc.**, a corporation organized in the State of Delaware, United States,
with its address at **600 Cleveland St. STE 393, Clearwater, FL
33755-4151, United States** ("EventOS", "JBD", "we" or the "Company").

In general terms, JBD may act:

-   As a **processor** with respect to the personal data that a customer
    organization uploads or processes through EventOS on its own behalf.
-   As a **controller** with respect to the data that JBD determines to
    process in order to administer accounts, subscriptions, billing,
    security, support, communications and operation of the service.

The customer organization is responsible for determining whether it has
a valid legal basis to enter and process personal data of its customers,
workers, suppliers or other third parties in EventOS.

**Privacy contact:** baileyai@realitynear.org

## 2. Scope

This Policy explains how we process personal data related to our
EventOS sites, applications and services. It does not replace each
customer organization's own privacy obligations toward the individuals
whose data it manages through the platform.

## 3. Data we may process

Depending on the features used, we may process:

-   **Account and organization:** name, email, organization, role,
    country, time zone, currency and account settings.
-   **Authentication and security:** account identifiers, sessions, IP
    addresses, access logs and security events. Credentials are managed
    through our authentication services and we do not store passwords in
    plain text.
-   **Customer Data:** information that the organization enters into
    EventOS, which may include customers, contacts, events, quotes,
    schedules, inventories, staff, expenses, files, communications and
    other data used in the contracted modules.
-   **Billing:** plan, subscription status, amounts, dates and
    transaction references. Full card data is processed by the payment
    provider and not by EventOS.
-   **Usage and diagnostics:** features used, technical logs, errors,
    browser, device and data reasonably necessary for security, support
    and performance.
-   **Support and communications:** messages and files that you send us
    when requesting support or contacting us.
-   **AI:** prompts, instructions, context and files that the user
    chooses to send to an artificial intelligence feature.

We do not seek to intentionally collect data from minors through
EventOS's own accounts. EventOS is directed primarily at organizations
and professional users of legal age.

## 4. Purposes

We may use the data to:

1.  Create, authenticate and administer accounts and organizations.
2.  Provide the contracted modules and features.
3.  Process and administer subscriptions and payments.
4.  Provide support and operational communications.
5.  Maintain security, prevent fraud, investigate incidents and
    protect the service.
6.  Diagnose errors and improve performance, reliability and product
    experience.
7.  Comply with legal, regulatory, accounting and tax obligations.
8.  Send marketing only where there is a valid legal basis and
    respecting the applicable opt-out choices.
9.  Process requests directed to AI features when the user uses them.

We do not sell personal data in exchange for money. If in the future
EventOS were to carry out an activity that legally qualified as a
"sale", "sharing" for behavioral advertising or targeted advertising, we
will update this Policy and offer the opt-out mechanisms required by
applicable law.

## 5. Legal bases

Where a regulation requires identifying a legal basis, the processing
may be based, as applicable, on:

-   performance of a contract or pre-contractual measures;
-   compliance with legal obligations;
-   legitimate interests, such as security, fraud prevention, support
    and reasonable improvement of the service, where such interests are
    not overridden by the applicable rights; or
-   consent, where required.

When we process Customer Data on behalf of an organization, the
organization normally determines the purpose and legal basis of that
processing.

## 6. Artificial Intelligence

Some EventOS features may use third-party AI models or services.
**Current providers:** Google (Gemini), Anthropic (Claude) and OpenAI.

When a user activates an AI feature, EventOS may transmit to the
provider the information necessary to execute the request, such as
prompts, context or selected files.

Retention, use for service improvement and any use for training by each
provider will depend on the configuration, product and contractual terms
in effect applicable to EventOS.

EventOS will clearly inform the user when they are interacting with an
AI feature where applicable. Generated content may be inaccurate and
must be reviewed by a person before being used for relevant decisions.

## 8. International transfers

JBD is incorporated in the United States and uses providers that may
process information in the United States or other regions. Where an
international transfer requires specific safeguards, we will use the
legally available mechanisms that apply, including contractual
agreements and transfer clauses offered by our providers where

## 9. Retention and deletion

We retain data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the
purposes described, legal obligations, dispute resolution and security.

As a V1 operating rule:

-   While an account is active, we will retain the data necessary to
    provide the service.
-   After termination, the organization will have **30 days**, where
    technically available and subject to legal or security
    restrictions, to request or complete a reasonable export of its
    Customer Data.
-   After that period, we may begin deleting Customer Data from active
    systems, subject to backup cycles, legal obligations and records we
    must retain.
-   Billing, security or audit records may be retained for different
    periods where there is a legal or legitimate need.

Deletion from backups may occur on a deferred basis according to
technical backup and recovery cycles.
