1. Who we are

EventOS is a software-as-a-service (SaaS) platform for managing the production and operation of events, operated by JBD Investment Corp Inc., a corporation organized in the State of Delaware, United States, with its address at 600 Cleveland St. STE 393, Clearwater, FL 33755-4151, United States ("EventOS", "JBD", "we" or the "Company").

In general terms, JBD may act:

  • As a processor with respect to the personal data that a customer organization uploads or processes through EventOS on its own behalf.
  • As a controller with respect to the data that JBD determines to process in order to administer accounts, subscriptions, billing, security, support, communications and operation of the service.

The customer organization is responsible for determining whether it has a valid legal basis to enter and process personal data of its customers, workers, suppliers or other third parties in EventOS.

Privacy contact: baileyai@realitynear.org

2. Scope

This Policy explains how we process personal data related to our EventOS sites, applications and services. It does not replace each customer organization's own privacy obligations toward the individuals whose data it manages through the platform.

3. Data we may process

Depending on the features used, we may process:

  • Account and organization: name, email, organization, role, country, time zone, currency and account settings.
  • Authentication and security: account identifiers, sessions, IP addresses, access logs and security events. Credentials are managed through our authentication services and we do not store passwords in plain text.
  • Customer Data: information that the organization enters into EventOS, which may include customers, contacts, events, quotes, schedules, inventories, staff, expenses, files, communications and other data used in the contracted modules.
  • Billing: plan, subscription status, amounts, dates and transaction references. Full card data is processed by the payment provider and not by EventOS.
  • Usage and diagnostics: features used, technical logs, errors, browser, device and data reasonably necessary for security, support and performance.
  • Support and communications: messages and files that you send us when requesting support or contacting us.
  • AI: prompts, instructions, context and files that the user chooses to send to an artificial intelligence feature.

We do not seek to intentionally collect data from minors through EventOS's own accounts. EventOS is directed primarily at organizations and professional users of legal age.

4. Purposes

We may use the data to:

  1. Create, authenticate and administer accounts and organizations.
  2. Provide the contracted modules and features.
  3. Process and administer subscriptions and payments.
  4. Provide support and operational communications.
  5. Maintain security, prevent fraud, investigate incidents and protect the service.
  6. Diagnose errors and improve performance, reliability and product experience.
  7. Comply with legal, regulatory, accounting and tax obligations.
  8. Send marketing only where there is a valid legal basis and respecting the applicable opt-out choices.
  9. Process requests directed to AI features when the user uses them.

We do not sell personal data in exchange for money. If in the future EventOS were to carry out an activity that legally qualified as a "sale", "sharing" for behavioral advertising or targeted advertising, we will update this Policy and offer the opt-out mechanisms required by applicable law.

5. Legal bases

Where a regulation requires identifying a legal basis, the processing may be based, as applicable, on:

  • performance of a contract or pre-contractual measures;
  • compliance with legal obligations;
  • legitimate interests, such as security, fraud prevention, support and reasonable improvement of the service, where such interests are not overridden by the applicable rights; or
  • consent, where required.

When we process Customer Data on behalf of an organization, the organization normally determines the purpose and legal basis of that processing.

6. Artificial Intelligence

Some EventOS features may use third-party AI models or services. Current providers: Google (Gemini), Anthropic (Claude) and OpenAI.

When a user activates an AI feature, EventOS may transmit to the provider the information necessary to execute the request, such as prompts, context or selected files.

Retention, use for service improvement and any use for training by each provider will depend on the configuration, product and contractual terms in effect applicable to EventOS.

EventOS will clearly inform the user when they are interacting with an AI feature where applicable. Generated content may be inaccurate and must be reviewed by a person before being used for relevant decisions.

8. International transfers

JBD is incorporated in the United States and uses providers that may process information in the United States or other regions. Where an international transfer requires specific safeguards, we will use the legally available mechanisms that apply, including contractual agreements and transfer clauses offered by our providers where applicable.

9. Retention and deletion

We retain data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described, legal obligations, dispute resolution and security.

As a V1 operating rule:

  • While an account is active, we will retain the data necessary to provide the service.
  • After termination, the organization will have 30 days, where technically available and subject to legal or security restrictions, to request or complete a reasonable export of its Customer Data.
  • After that period, we may begin deleting Customer Data from active systems, subject to backup cycles, legal obligations and records we must retain.
  • Billing, security or audit records may be retained for different periods where there is a legal or legitimate need.

Deletion from backups may occur on a deferred basis according to technical backup and recovery cycles.

10. Privacy rights

Depending on your jurisdiction and your relationship with JBD, you may have rights of access, information, correction, updating, deletion/cancellation, objection, portability, restriction of processing, withdrawal of consent and opt-out of certain uses.

To exercise them: baileyai@realitynear.org.

We may request reasonable information to verify identity and authority. We will respond within the period required by applicable law. When JBD acts solely as a processor for a customer organization, we may refer the request to that organization or collaborate with it.

Where applicable law provides for an appeal procedure against a decision on privacy rights, we will inform the requester how to exercise it.

11. Privacy in the United States and Delaware

Residents of U.S. states with applicable privacy laws may have additional rights, including to know/access, correct, delete, obtain a copy and, where applicable, opt out of the sale of data, targeted advertising or certain automated profiling.

The specific applicability of each law depends, among other factors, on the jurisdiction, data volume, nature of the processing and legal exemptions.

12. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures proportionate to the risk, including access controls, encryption in transit, logical separation between organizations and database security mechanisms where applicable.

No service can guarantee absolute security. We will handle incidents and notifications to customers, individuals or authorities in accordance with applicable legal obligations.

13. Cookies and similar technologies

The use of cookies and similar technologies is explained in our Cookie Policy. Non-essential technologies will be activated in accordance with applicable consent requirements.

14. Changes

We may update this Policy to reflect legal, technical or service changes. We will publish the update date and, where applicable, notify material changes by reasonable means.
