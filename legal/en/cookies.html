1. What cookies are

Cookies and similar technologies allow a site or application to remember information, maintain a session, protect an account or measure use of the service.

2. Necessary technologies

EventOS uses strictly necessary technologies to:

  • authenticate users and maintain sessions;
  • protect accounts and prevent abuse;
  • remember essential settings;
  • allow secure handoff between modules;
  • maintain technical functions necessary to provide the service.

These technologies are not used for the purpose of behavioral advertising.

3. Analytics

As of the date of this version, EventOS does not use analytics tools or advertising or retargeting technologies. If in the future we incorporate an analytics tool, we will update this section and the following table with the exact name, provider, purpose, duration and technologies used, and we will enable the prior consent required by applicable law before its use.

5. Preferences

Strictly necessary technologies cannot be disabled because they are necessary for functions requested by the user.

If in the future we incorporate non-essential technologies that require consent, we will offer appropriate controls to accept, reject or configure such technologies before their activation, in accordance with applicable law.

You may also delete or block cookies from your browser, although doing so may affect essential functions.

6. Signals and opt-out mechanisms

Where an applicable law requires recognizing universal opt-out mechanisms or equivalent technical signals for activities covered by that law, EventOS will endeavor to implement the corresponding mechanism. This clause does not mean that we currently sell data or carry out targeted advertising.

7. Changes

We will update this Policy when the technologies used or the applicable obligations change.
